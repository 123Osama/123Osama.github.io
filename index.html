Hello, I'm Osama

I am a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Bangladesh Army University of Science and Technology (BAUST), Bangladesh, and currently pursuing an M.Sc. in CSE at RUET. My research interests include Large Language Models (LLMs), Natural Language Processing (NLP), Machine Unlearning, and trustworthy AI for low-resource languages, particularly Bangla.

Official Contact: osama_cse@baust.edu.bd
