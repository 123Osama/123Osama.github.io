Research Interests

My research interests focus on advancing trustworthy and efficient AI systems, particularly for low-resource and morphologically rich languages such as Bangla. My current areas of interest include:

  • Large Language Models (LLMs)
  • Machine Unlearning
  • Natural Language Processing (NLP)
  • Bangla Natural Language Processing

Ongoing Research Projects

I am currently involved in the following research projects:

  • Developing a morphology-aware benchmark for evaluating machine unlearning in Bangla Large Language Models. [M.Sc. Thesis]
  • Investigating efficient evaluation strategies for trustworthy machine unlearning in low-resource languages. [Research Project]
